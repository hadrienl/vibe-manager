iPhone companion (alpha)

Follow your sessions from the iPhone and answer your agents' requests, even away from the Mac. The companion is in alpha: the iPhone app is distributed through TestFlight, and it needs an Apple silicon Mac.

Install the alpha

  1. On the Mac, open Settings › iPhone Companion and turn on Use Vibe Manager from your iPhone; the helper ships with Vibe Manager and is off by default.
  2. How to install the alpha… opens the instructions to join the TestFlight test and install the app on the iPhone.
  3. Open the app on the iPhone, with the same iCloud account as the Mac: it shows in the list of iPhones. Remove… takes it off.

On an Intel Mac, the page says the companion is not available.

What the companion does

  • The list of your sessions, their status and what each agent is doing, grouped as on the Mac.
  • Notifications when an agent needs you: Allow (after Face ID or the passcode) or Deny without opening the app.
  • Questions, including those that ask several at once, which you answer from the iPhone.

The web view

In a conversation, the Web View button opens the session's web view. It shows the Mac's active page and follows it, and the tabs menu switches between the Mac's tabs. The subtitle says In sync with the Mac. The Mac and the iPhone don't share a session: each has its own cookies.

  • Only your own navigations are sent to the Mac, meaning an address you type or a link you touch. Redirects, reloads and form answers stay on the iPhone.
  • On a sign-in page (GitHub, GitLab, Google, Microsoft, Apple, Okta, Auth0, Keycloak…), the view detaches by itself: the Not Synced badge shows in the address bar, and the iPhone stops following the Mac and sends it nothing. It attaches again by itself when the sign-in lands back on a page of the site. Touch the badge to attach it sooner.
  • Stop Syncing detaches the view by hand. It stays detached until you touch Not Synced or pick a tab.
  • Open in Safari opens the page shown in Safari, or in the app its address opens, such as GitHub. It is dimmed on the Mac's own pages, which only the Mac can reach.

Passkeys don't work in the web view. Sign in with a password, or open the page in Safari.

No server

The Mac and the iPhone talk through CloudKit, in your private iCloud: no Vibe Manager server between them. Only iPhones whose key is published in your iCloud are let in.
