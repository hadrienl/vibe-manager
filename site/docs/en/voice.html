Dictation and reading aloud

Dictate your messages and have the agent's replies read to you. Both run on your Mac, with models downloaded once: nothing you say or hear leaves the machine.

The Voice page of the settings.

Dictate a message

Click the microphone beside the + of the composer and speak; a second click stops the recording. The text is transcribed on the Mac by Whisper and inserted at the cursor, without being sent: read it over, then Return. Esc drops the recording.

The first time, a bubble offers to download the model. Once the download is done, nothing records on its own: a notification and a sound let you know, and it is up to you to click the microphone again.

Listen to a reply

The ▶ button under a reply of the agent, or Read Aloud in its contextual menu, reads it. A pill stops the reading; only one reply is read at a time. Code blocks are skipped, and a table is read cell by cell.

Settings › Voice

Speech Model
Whisper Large v3 Turbo (the default, 626 MB), which understands technical terms best, or Whisper Small (216 MB), for a Mac with little memory. The page shows the space taken on disk, and lets you download or delete each model.
Language
Automatic, or one of the app's languages to force the dictation language.
Reading Aloud
The voice, among nine, and the reading language. The reading model weighs about 1 GB.
