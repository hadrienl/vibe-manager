Themes and avatars

Dress the conversation in a theme of your own, give the floating panel an avatar, and choose the badges your sessions wear.

A personal theme, made by describing it to an agent.

Conversation themes

In Settings › Conversation, choose a theme for the conversation view: System Light, System Dark, Paper, Night, Terminal, High Contrast, or one of your own. With Follow light and dark mode, you pick one theme for each mode, and the conversation follows macOS. Every built-in theme meets the WCAG contrast ratios.

Themes, display and technical details, with a live preview.

The same page sets the accent colour, the fonts of messages and code, the text size, the density, whether your messages are bubbles or lines, and how much of the technical details is unfolded. Restore Defaults puts it all back.

A session can also have a theme of its own, to tell it apart at a glance: choose it in More Options when you create it, or later with Session › Change Conversation Theme…, the inspector's header or its row's context menu. Follow Settings brings it back to the theme above, and ⌘Z undoes the change. A session template can suggest a theme.

Create your own theme

  1. Click Create My Theme and describe what you want: “a calm forest at dawn, moss green, warm paper for my messages”.
  2. Click Generate with Claude Code or Generate with Codex. The agent answers with the 27 colours of a theme, held to the same contrasts as the built-in ones. The version is shown at once, on the preview and in every conversation, without changing your settings.
  3. Ask for changes: each one is a new version, and you can go back to any of them.
  4. Name it and click Save and Apply the Theme.

A theme can carry a picture behind the conversation: give the address of an image, or describe it and let Codex draw it (one or two minutes). The theme says how veiled and blurred it is. Your themes can be exported as a .zip or deleted from their card's menu.

Generation runs once with your agent's own account, outside any session, from an empty temporary folder, without tools, MCP servers or web access. Only your description is sent; the answer is checked and used as colours, nothing else.

Avatars

The floating panel presents requests with an animated avatar. Settings › Notifications › Manage Avatars… holds your library of avatars:

  • Create a New Avatar: describe it, and Codex draws its ten expressions — neutral, speaking, blinking, pleased, surprised, thinking, worried… — on one sheet, in about a minute and a half. A single expression can be drawn again, or all of them.
  • Import an Archive…: a .zip exported from Vibe Manager, or ten images named after the expressions, drawn by hand.
  • Use This Avatar puts it on the panel. Avatars can be renamed, duplicated, exported (with or without their description) and deleted.

What an agent draws is a draft until you keep it, and every image is checked and re-encoded by the app before it is used.

Badges

Each session wears a badge: a symbol on a colour, or its project's icon. Settings › Badges lists the symbols and colours offered by the badge picker: add your own, reorder them (⌥← ⌥→), remove them, or go back to the default list. A colour is only accepted if the white symbol stays readable on it (a contrast of at least 3:1).
